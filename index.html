How I work

Curiosity first.
Then organised action.

The approach comes from years of outreach and production work: understand the person or project before asking for anything.

01

Understand the brief

Clarify the goal, audience, constraints, resources, roles and what successful delivery looks like.

02

Research the possibilities

Find people, stories, routes of introduction, practical options and informed angles worth pursuing.

03

Coordinate the moving parts

Manage contact, schedules, records, teams, logistics and sensible follow-up without unnecessary noise.

04

Deliver and document

Keep stakeholders informed, respond to obstacles and leave the project with usable outputs and clear next steps.
