Why this capability is credible

PHATSO — commercial audio plugin launch

Developed and launched a seven-plugin music technology suite, with creator positioning, product design, demos, testing, pricing, Lemon Squeezy checkout, promotion and paid customers documented in the master career record.

Harmonic Reset — digital wellness audio offer

Developed the guided audio experience, product mechanism, marketing funnel and commercial presentation. The ClickBank-oriented offer is part of this product's route to market; current listing/payment status is not independently verified.

Outreach Mania — AI-assisted outreach product

Created a research-led outreach application and marketing website, iterating desktop builds, usability, outreach logic and commercial licensing concepts. The existing website describes checkout/download as coming soon, so sales are not claimed.

Build & Reach — service business

Built and deployed a site selling practical digital, website and outreach services, with clear packages and customer-facing propositions.

Production Desk — service launch with real outreach

Built the live production-services site and launched personalised international prospecting. An outreach campaign is evidence of market action, not yet evidence of sales.

Founder Lab — pipeline of concepts and prototypes

Created and maintained a portfolio hub of numerous business experiments and working prototypes with varied maturity, including Founder Pocket and other idea-to-product tools.

Scope: PHATSO is documented as a launched product with paid customers. Harmonic Reset is a product/offer, Outreach Mania a developing product, Production Desk a live service with outreach, and Founder Lab contains prototypes. They are not presented as equally mature revenue-generating companies.
