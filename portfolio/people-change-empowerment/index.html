Work and evidence

Why this capability is credible

Moment Mastery — behavioural framework

Created a practical framework focused on self-belief, clarity under pressure, identity, decision-making, leadership rhythm, accountability and constructive action. The published work examines what people do in defining moments.

Creative empowerment in India

Developed programmes to help underprivileged young people create, record and publish music, connecting skills, confidence and digital participation with new possibilities.

Youth mentoring in South Central Los Angeles

Worked with young people using music, relationships, communication and practical creative engagement to build confidence and constructive opportunities.

Social and humanitarian music

Mobilised more than forty musicians for a Red Cross-linked humanitarian music project and created music initiatives connected to peace, diversity and cross-cultural inclusion.

People-centred creative projects

Designed Producing Human Encounters as an equal-participation conversation format rather than a guru-and-audience event. Programme development is not represented as a completed commissioned series.

Scope: Moment Mastery is a self-developed framework, not a regulated clinical intervention. Community work and mentoring are described as they appear in the career record, without claiming licensed counselling or formal care qualifications.
