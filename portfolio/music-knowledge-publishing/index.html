The proposition

What I bring

Beyond making recordings, I investigate the music, musicians, techniques, cultural histories and human stories behind them. The Music Knowledge Library turns the WORLD POP research process into structured, published editorial material that readers can explore independently.

Work and evidence

Why this capability is credible

Published Music Knowledge Library

A dedicated online resource covering musicians, songs, recording craft, music psychology, songwriting, culture and the human experience of listening.

WORLD POP Episode 2 research series

Published connected explorations of John Barry, Burt Bacharach, David Foster, Gurrumul, Neil Diamond, Christopher Cross and Ernie Watts, linking musical details to wider cultural stories.

Recording and voice education

Published articles on microphone technique, recording rooms, vocal practice and the practical craft of shaping a musical performance.

Music psychology and culture

Structured articles explore nostalgia, memory, musical chills, rhythm, syncopation and why songs connect across generations and borders.

First-hand historical perspectives

Connects professional experience with archival and editorial research into Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Sydney Opera House sessions, orchestral projects and recording practice.

Scope: This is an editorial knowledge and research library, distinct from a licensable audio-track catalogue. Being indexed or published does not itself establish independent academic peer review of all entries.
