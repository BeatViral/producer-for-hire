Foundations · 1997–2001

A multi-million-selling collaboration, then an original album across borders.

Only One (1997) — Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan

Mahmood's album collaboration with qawwali legend Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan was reported to have sold more than six million copies worldwide. An early example of building original music across South Asian traditions and international production environments.

Panah (2001) — featuring Nandini Srikar

Mahmood wrote, performed and produced Panah, released by MagnaSound in India and featuring singer and musician Nandini Srikar. The album blended Sufi and Indian musical influences with production techniques developed in Los Angeles; its recordings were subsequently licensed to Sony Music Entertainment India.

International production · Urdu-language chart milestone

Ginoo — produced with Gary Grant.

Mahmood worked with legendary American trumpeter and producer Gary Grant, who produced Ginoo (also styled Ginno). Contemporary reporting places the Urdu-language recording within the US Billboard Top 10 in 2020; published accounts differ on its precise highest position.

Gary Grant — the Jerry Hey Horns, Quincy Jones and Michael Jackson

Gary Grant worked alongside Jerry Hey and became one of Los Angeles's most sought-after studio trumpeters. His documented credits include Michael Jackson, Quincy Jones, Barbra Streisand, Whitney Houston, Celine Dion, Earth, Wind & Fire, Toto, Frank Sinatra and Elton John. He also co-produced a Latin Grammy-winning Arturo Sandoval recording. Grant's production of Ginoo reflects a professional relationship Mahmood developed from their first meeting in Los Angeles in 1991.

Direct testimony, 24 August 2018: In an email to Mahmood, Grant praised the mastered mix and wrote, “your vocal sounds superb.” He confirmed his own contributions on trumpet, flugelhorn, cornet and piccolo trumpet, and credited Charla Blevins on French horn. He declined payment for their work, requesting proper musician credits, and described his contributions as a donation in support of Mahmood’s musical career.

The email is private correspondence supplied by Mahmood Matloob. Only short excerpts are quoted here; no private contact information is published.

Orchestras of the World · 2009–2020

A decade of orchestral leadership behind a Billboard No. 1.

The 2019 recording with the Willoughby Symphony Orchestra was the culmination of work Mahmood began in 2009, not a single recording session. He originated the concept, wrote and developed repertoire, explored professional orchestral possibilities across Australia, Singapore, Thailand, China and Europe, and assembled the specialist team to realise it.

Development and repertoire

Ten years exploring orchestras, conductors, arrangers and musicians; writing and developing original cross-cultural orchestral material.

Specialist recruitment

Engaged international arranger and musical director Doug Emery for scores and demos, and David Griffin for arranging, orchestration and conducting.

2019 recording

Secured Willoughby Symphony Orchestra musicians and Fox Studios recording facilities; coordinated the artistic and technical production, including a multi-camera crew.

Post-production and US release

Oversaw audio mixing and video editing, engaged US promoters, prepared release and chart-reporting documentation, and liaised with Billboard.

Outcome

No. 1 · Billboard Classical Crossover Albums (2020)

The album Mahmood Khan with Willoughby Symphony Orchestra entered Billboard's Classical Crossover Albums chart and subsequently reached No. 1. Its international release followed a substantial programme of artistic preparation, recording, post-production and promotion.

The calibre of the creative team

International expertise, brought together around original work.

Doug Emery — arranger, producer, keyboardist and musical director

Mahmood engaged Doug Emery to develop scores and demos for the orchestral project. Emery's reported career credits include work with Barry Gibb, Barbra Streisand, Madonna and Chayanne. His collaboration brought considerable international studio and arranging experience to an original cross-cultural repertoire.

“Working with Mahmood has been a unique experience.”

In his published endorsement, Emery also praised Mahmood's ability to connect spirituality and emotion in his music.

David Griffin arranged, orchestrated and conducted music for the Willoughby Symphony live and studio sessions. His wider experience includes conducting youth, school, community and professional ensembles, making his contribution especially relevant to Mahmood's continuing interest in developing orchestral opportunities.

“Mahmood’s music is full of positivity”

Griffin's published remarks also warmly described arranging and orchestrating the songs and conducting the Willoughby Symphony Orchestra.

Musical originality from regional Australia

WORLD/POP — developing and presenting a cross-cultural sound in Ballina.

Mahmood developed WORLD/POP as a musical approach joining global melodic traditions with American funk, pop, South American rhythmic influences and elements of bluegrass and country. In 2022 he promoted a live presentation in Ballina, Northern Rivers NSW, with a six-piece band and a planned television recording for the documentary Journey of a New Sound.

An original genre proposition, not merely a concert

Contemporary coverage described the Ballina event as the introduction of a new world/pop genre. Mahmood has described the project as developed with Channel 9 involvement; publicly available launch coverage confirms the planned television filming but does not independently establish the exact terms of Channel 9's participation. The claim is therefore presented here without assigning Channel 9 formal co-producer status.

WORLD/POP, the musical concept, is distinct from WORLD POP with Mahmood Khan, the later radio programme exploring the music, stories and cultures behind popular music. The radio programme is also being developed as a network connecting musicians, composers, educators and cultural leaders.

Jazz-influenced international collaboration

Imaginary Friend — original recordings with experienced US producers.

The 2021 Imaginary Friend jazz EP reached No. 1 on the US iTunes Jazz chart. Mahmood identifies songwriter-producer J. Will Michaels and producer David Jones as collaborators in this work.

J. Will Michaels — songwriter and producer

Also publicly credited as J. Michaels, a Los Angeles music producer whose reported professional credits include Michael Jackson and Brandy. In a published interview, Michaels praised Mahmood's songwriting and original way of blending musical influences.

David Jones — songwriter and producer

American producer whose reported credits include Elton John, Bon Jovi, Garth Brooks and Eddie Murphy. Jones publicly endorsed Mahmood's artistic development and commitment to musical expression, describing his pride in being part of the journey.

The reported wider artist credits are attributed to published interviews; detailed track-level songwriting and production splits for Imaginary Friend should be confirmed against release metadata before being presented as formal credits.

Continuing the work

From achieved excellence to future musicians.

The experience of finding exceptional collaborators, building original repertoire and coordinating professional productions now informs Mahmood's Improv Symphony concept and the professional network he is developing through WORLD POP radio. Conversations with music leaders and conservatoriums explore emerging-musician opportunities, improvisation, artistic confidence and cross-cultural collaboration; these initiatives remain in development rather than operating as funded national programmes.
