Artistry at the heart of the work

The songwriter, the performer and the sound.

Decades of original composition, recording and live performance underpin the wider production, cultural and commercial career. Mahmood Khan's music is the source—not a subsection—of much of that work. The catalogue includes more than 200 original songs and a continuing interest in how different musical cultures can meet without losing their identities.

Selected recorded and live milestones

An artist with a documented body of work.

Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan

Multi-million-selling album collaboration with Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, part of an international recording journey spanning South Asia, the United States and Australia.

Billboard No. 1

Collaboration with Willoughby Symphony Orchestra associated with a No. 1 on Billboard's Classical Crossover Albums chart, uniting original songs and orchestral performance.

ARIA No. 1

“Like the River,” recorded live at Sydney Opera House, reached No. 1 on the ARIA Physical Singles chart.

Performance and broadcast

Live music with bands in Pakistan and Australia, orchestral appearances, Sydney Opera House recordings and a Channel 9 performance of “Merry Go Round.”

International musical relationships

Recorded and worked across musical genres and cultures, including historically documented work with Coolio and Damon Thomas.

Original projects and new music

Continuing songwriting and recording, alongside the proposed Improv Symphony concept. Proposals and future performances are not described as confirmed bookings.
